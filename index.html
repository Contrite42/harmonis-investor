Six licenses. One rhythm.

Harmonis turns a bar's six music-licensing bills into one predictable weekly or monthly payment, and handles everything in between. The product is live at harmonis.io; this walkthrough shows it.

The problem

Every bar that plays music owes six different companies.

ASCAP, BMI, SESAC, GMR, AllTrack and Pro Music Rights each license a different catalog, bill on their own schedule, and can come after a venue independently.

The bills land once a year, next to liquor license, insurance and health permit renewals. Playing music without the right license is copyright infringement, and willful infringement can carry statutory damages up to $150,000 per song.1

What Harmonis is

One agreement. We handle all six.

The same model that fixed commercial insurance premiums: someone carries the lump sum so the venue doesn't have to. Harmonis does it for music licensing.

  1. Quote in minutes. Plain-English questions; the engine prices every PRO from its own rate schedule.
  2. Sign once. One online agreement with a Power of Attorney and Letters of Agency.
  3. We pay the PROs. Harmonis pays the six organizations and owns renewals, rate changes and correspondence.1
  4. One payment, live proof. Weekly or monthly by bank transfer, plus a QR-verifiable certificate.
  1. Upfront PRO payment is the operating model; it is funded by the working-capital line in the raise and starts when live payments switch on.

How it makes money

A 10% service fee on a bill venues already have to pay.

10% of the venue's annual PRO bill + $1.99/month platform fee, paid by bank transfer. A card option adds a 3.5% surcharge.1

The licensing money passes through to the PROs. Harmonis earns the fee, and the fee grows as PRO rates rise.
