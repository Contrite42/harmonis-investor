The problem

Music drives the check. Paying for it is stuck in the past.

Planned payment flow

Calculate daily. Collect weekly. Reconcile separately.

The proposed flow calculates allocations without moving money, collects weekly and keeps evidence for amounts owed, collected and paid to each PRO.

Design, not a verified live ACH service. The illustrative $4.12 × 7 = $28.84 weekly total is not a live debit.

Daily calculation

Record a timestamped venue allocation; calculation itself moves no money.

Advance notice

Show the planned debit and notify the owner before collection. Delivery timing needs validation.

Weekly ACH collection

Combine entries into one debit. Provider activation and actual processing costs need validation.

Failure handling

Reconcile failed collections, hold affected payouts and route follow-up. ACH returns require end-to-end testing.

§8.3 Operating economics

Low prices need low acquisition and servicing costs.

Keep pricing affordable and use automation to reduce routine account work. Section 8.3 of the business plan sets an operating goal, not a measured near-zero cost claim.

Automation

SEO/referrals aim to lower acquisition cost; onboarding and reminders aim to reduce routine servicing.

People handle exceptions

Plan for support, collections and PRO account management. Measure workload before setting staffing ratios.

Measure contribution

Track retention, support time, processing cost and contribution margin. Legal, marketing and infrastructure remain costs.
